Welcome to Rebel Nature, the podcast that explores how Nature can rewire our brains and change our perception of everyday things. Modern life too often feels at odds with the one outside my window. I explore how Nature is expressed, or too often omitted, from our modern world. And also, how Nature serves as a beautiful redemption from life’s rigid and grid-like expectations.
